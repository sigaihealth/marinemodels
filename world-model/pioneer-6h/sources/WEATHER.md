# Atmospheric and sea-state observations

Run `python build_weather.py --output data-weather` with Python 3.11 or newer. The output includes 22,656 hourly issue times from April 1, 2024 through October 31, 2026 (UTC). The raw NOAA National Data Buoy Center standard meteorological archive URLs and SHA-256 hashes are in `weather_provenance.json`. Annual compressed files cover 2024 and 2025. The 2026 records came from monthly files. NDBC’s current-year monthly addresses can rotate after annual consolidation. The derived CSVs pin the data actually used.

**Features at issue time.** For wind, the OOI Southern Surface Mooring (41083, about 35 km from Pioneer Central) provides observed speed in m/s and meteorological direction from true north. The builder converts each to eastward and northward wind velocity, then averages the valid vectors in the previous hour. For waves, the Oregon Inlet buoy (44095, about 29 km from Pioneer Central) supplies the latest valid significant wave height in metres and, when available, the dominant period in seconds. Each hourly bin excludes its lower endpoint and includes its issue time. Original reporting times and counts are included. Neither product is observed at the target CTD. These archive times identify when measurements were made. Real-time posting and later QC latencies were unavailable. [Southern mooring](https://www.ndbc.noaa.gov/station_page.php?station=41083) · [Oregon Inlet wave buoy](https://www.ndbc.noaa.gov/station_page.php?station=44095) · [NDBC field definitions](https://www.ndbc.noaa.gov/measdes.shtml)

**Coverage.** 20,550 hours had wind vectors and 22,485 had wave height. Both were present at 20,394 hours. The parser drops NDBC missing sentinels and implausible directions, and retains zero wind as a zero vector. Empty CSV cells denote missing measurements.

**Held-out event definitions.** Two or more consecutive hourly significant wave heights of at least 3.0 m define a qualifying episode. Episodes merge transitively if successive episode peaks lie less than 72 hours apart or if their windows (12 hours before onset, 24 hours after the last qualifying hour) overlap. The strongest episode peak names the merged storm, with the first hourly bin winning ties. Start and end in `storms.csv` are inclusive. Disjoint windows and a minimum 72-hour peak separation are asserted. The selection is based solely on wave observations. Retrospective storm membership is an evaluation stratum and must never enter a predictor. There are 34 resulting events (5 in 2024, 18 in 2025 and 11 in 2026) before checking other instruments. Gianna peaks at 6.51 m in the February 1, 2026 11:00 UTC bin (native reading at 10:56 UTC).
