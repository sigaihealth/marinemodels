# Pioneer upper-water currents

The OOI Central Surface Mooring carries a single-point velocity meter on the same nominal 7 m frame as the CTD. The four instrument deployments in the pinned OOI asset table share the CTD deployment dates and positions. Some deployments are about 1 km farther west than others. Select this instrument for its sensor geometry before looking at any forecast errors. [OOI platform](https://oceanobservatories.org/site/cp10cnsm/) · [Current meter observations and metadata](https://erddap.dataexplorer.oceanobservatories.org/erddap/tabledap/ooi-cp10cnsm-rid26-04-velpta000.html) · [OOI deployment table](https://github.com/oceanobservatories/asset-management/blob/4466ceaa325eb23e125eff7c9850dd000fc79aef/deployment/CP10CNSM_Deploy.csv)

The positive components point east and north in metres per second. Use only paired, finite velocity measurements that have passed OOI's aggregate quality flag (1) on both axes. Exclude ambiguous deployment overlaps, times outside deployment and any available measured depth outside 3–12 m. When pressure-derived depth is missing, retain the nominal frame depth. The depth field was missing for 37,460 native records after other screens. For each hourly forecast, take the vector mean of at least two distinct readings in the previous 75 to 15 minutes, excluding the older endpoint. Require the latest reading to be at most 30 minutes old. Preserve its exact timestamp and the start of the aggregation. Keep all contributing observations and the issue time within one deployment.

The 15-minute lag is a guard for temporal representativeness of a native report. The observed native interval was usually 15 minutes (74,480 consecutive intervals in the archive were 900 seconds). The ERDDAP metadata do not establish the center/end timestamp convention for each native instrument ensemble or the real-time receipt of its quality flags. This is a hindcast using archived measurements and quality control. The processing leaves gaps when no eligible vector exists.

The frozen extraction requested data through November 26, 2026 at 02:00 UTC. The published stream ended November 25 at 04:00 UTC. It supplied 18,502 eligible hourly vectors from April 3, 2024 at 15:00 UTC to November 25, 2026 at 04:00 UTC. There were 74,564 native rows. Of these, 553 fell outside a unique deployment and one available measured depth was out of bounds. The four instrument serials and per-query source URLs and SHA-256 digests are recorded in `current_provenance.json`. `currents.csv` has SHA-256 `d5f3cf9f1257a924c67eef0dc2664d5e6d71b8cd0272e34eab08df08d8a1131c`.

Run `python fetch_currents.py --output currents-data` with Python 3.11 or newer to acquire the records. A future live request can include later backfill. Use the frozen input for reproducibility.
