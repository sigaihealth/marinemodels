# Duck astronomical tide

NOAA CO-OPS station 8651370 at Duck, North Carolina (36.1833° N, 75.7467° W) is approximately 62 km from the Pioneer Central Surface Mooring's microscope intake. The series describes the coastal astronomical tide, which provides regional timing for the shelf model. Local coastal and offshore tidal amplitudes and phases can differ. [Station metadata](https://api.tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov/mdapi/prod/webapi/stations/8651370.json) · [Duck tide predictions](https://tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov/noaatidepredictions.html?id=8651370)

The retrieval uses `product=predictions`, `interval=h`, `units=metric`, `time_zone=gmt` and `datum=MLLW`. `time` identifies the exact UTC hour and `tide_m` is the predicted height in metres relative to mean lower low water. The station publishes 37 harmonic constituents. NOAA lists the station tidal datum epoch as 1983–2001, accepted in 2011. The archive covers March 31, 2024 through November 26, 2026 inclusive, providing margins for both lags and forecast leads. All 23,304 hours have exactly one finite model value. Heights range from −0.227 to 1.494 m. [Data API](https://api.tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov/api/prod/) · [Harmonic constituents](https://api.tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov/mdapi/prod/webapi/stations/8651370/harcon.json?units=metric) · [Station datums](https://tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov/datums.html?id=8651370)

A forecast may use the astronomical tide at its issue time and at subsequent lead times because harmonic predictions can be calculated in advance. The input contains only the tide-prediction product. Storm water-level observations and observed-minus-predicted residuals are excluded from these features. NOAA's current historical prediction response does not carry an archived issue timestamp or a constituent version for each past forecast. Use the frozen inputs to reproduce this hindcast. [NOAA tide prediction method](https://tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov/about_harmonic_constituents.html)

Run `python fetch_tides.py --output PATH` to reconstruct `tides.csv` and `tide_provenance.json`. The script checks consecutiveness, UTC alignment, expected range, finite numeric heights and metadata. The provenance records exact request URLs, source hashes and the CSV checksum.
