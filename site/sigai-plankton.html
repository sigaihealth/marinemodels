      How well does the classifier transfer?

      Six research collections provided expert annotations for 28 of the classifier’s 101 categories. Across new acquisitions from four collections used in training, the original first choice matched the reference label on 0.3% of crops. The source-specific comparison matched it on 25.1%. Two entire collections were reserved for transfer testing. A separate Tara Pacific test provided evidence for three additional taxonomic categories and one historical label.

      Results by collection, data sources and limitations

      Can the classifier distinguish bubbles from larvae in VPR images?

      We tested a separate research classifier on Video Plankton Recorder images from two cruises held out from training. Arctic bubble detections rose from 130 to 146 of 220, with gains varying by acquisition. On a Canadian cruise, the model kept 241 of 250 larvae out of the bubble category, up from 64. It kept all 14 Arctic amphipods but labeled each as “other organism.”

      On two newly reserved Fram Strait cruises, retraining removed 106 ostracod false amphipod calls and retained 2 of the 3 Themisto detections added by the earlier ZooScan model.

      Arctic amphipods by instrument

      The VPR models recognized one of six expert-confirmed Themisto frames on two Healy acquisitions and kept 49 of 50 bubble detections. On two new Arctic cruises, a model trained for ZooScan recognized 75 additional Themisto and made 20 additional false amphipod calls on controls.
