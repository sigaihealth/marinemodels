How well did the reference comparison work?

On 148 held-out WHOI crops, the panel suggested a group for 82 and matched the expert label on 77. It recommended review for 66. Four of the 60 crops outside its four groups received a suggestion. These results describe the optional reference comparison.

      How well does the classifier transfer?

      Six research collections provided expert annotations for 28 of the classifier’s 101 categories. Across new acquisitions from four collections used in training, the original first choice matched the reference label on 0.3% of crops. The source-specific comparison matched it on 25.1%. The study also tested two entirely held-out collections.
