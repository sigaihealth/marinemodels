How OC3M works

The model divides the larger blue-band reflectance by green reflectance, takes the base-10 logarithm, and applies the polynomial fitted for MODIS. These are the R2022 coefficients published in NASA’s November 2023 algorithm document.

x = log₁₀(max(Rrs₄₄₃, Rrs₄₈₈) / Rrs₅₄₇)
log₁₀(Chl) = 0.26294 − 2.64669x + 1.28364x² + 1.08209x³ − 1.76828x⁴

The OC3 routine accepts a blue-to-green ratio strictly between 0.21 and 30. The 488 nm and 547 nm values must be positive, and the 443 nm value must exceed −0.001 sr⁻¹. The routine bounds its output to 0.001–1,000 mg/m³. These numerical checks cannot detect cloud contamination or correct a coastal scene affected by sediment or dissolved organic matter.

NASA’s operational R2022 chlor_a product also uses a color-index estimate, blending it with OC3M between 0.25 and 0.35 mg/m³ of color-index chlorophyll. This calculator runs the OC3M component alone. Compare it to field chlorophyll matched by time and location when evaluating a retrieval.
