Source and interpretation

Use the per-frame and per-track measures for this dive. Counting an expert individual as reached measures detection coverage. Evaluating identity continuity or human review time requires a separate experiment. The earlier Ventana V4289 and OOI results are available in the pilot reviewer.

Ground truth was initialized with MBARI’s 452k detector and ByteTrack and then manually reviewed, with missed organisms added and boxes adjusted. An unmatched model box is a candidate for visual review. Benchmark methods
