What the lines mean

GLONET predicts daily means on a ¼° grid. Day 1 is the UTC day after the archive’s forecast reference date. These historical runs cover January and July 2024.

GLO12 persistence repeats the previous day’s ocean analysis on its 1/12° grid. Both curves use the same geographic coordinates and depth. Their difference measures how far the forecast moves from that starting state. Forecast accuracy requires independent observations.

Forecast output is from Mercator Ocean International’s OceanBench. The persistence values contain E.U. Copernicus Marine Service Information. This subset samples and rounds the archived values.
