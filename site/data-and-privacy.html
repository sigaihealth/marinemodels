Classifying a crop

Choosing a file displays its name in your browser. Selecting Classify crop sends the PNG bytes, including any embedded metadata, to the SIG.AI service on Vercel. Use a microscope crop you have permission to process and remove personal or sensitive information before sending it.

The application returns predictions without saving the submitted crop or its prediction results. This applies to your own PNGs and the built-in examples. The optional reference and collection settings change how the model ranks the image. A recommendation for expert review appears in your result; you choose whether to share it with an expert.

Downloading the prediction JSON saves a copy on your device. It includes an image fingerprint and, for your own upload, the file name. Review it before sharing.

Contributing an image
for research

After classifying your own image, you can separately give SIG.AI permission to store its pixels and use them to train and evaluate plankton classifiers. Contribute only if you hold the rights or have permission for both uses. Research contributions began with this release on November 26, 2026 (UTC). Built-in examples are excluded.

What is stored. The contribution service re-encodes the image to discard the original PNG metadata. It saves the resulting crop with a receipt identifier, the consent version and timing information for expiry. The contribution record excludes the uploaded file name, IP address and classifier predictions.

Who has access. SIG.AI restricts the research collection to authorized project personnel working on the classifier or administering its storage. Vercel hosts it in a private Blob store in the Washington, D.C., United States region (iad1). Individual contributions have no public download link.

Retention. A contribution expires 28 days after submission. A daily cleanup removes expired records. You can remove yours sooner using its receipt.

Withdrawing a contribution

Download the private receipt when you contribute. To delete the stored crop, open the withdrawal section of the classifier, select the receipt and confirm removal. Anyone with the receipt can remove that contribution. Keep it private.

The receipt contains a deletion token. The image fingerprint in an ordinary prediction download identifies the input but does not authorize deletion. If you lose the receipt, email ocean@sig.ai for help. Include the receipt identifier if you have it. We may need more information to locate the record and verify your request. Keep the secret token out of email and public issues.

Withdrawal deletes the stored crop. To prevent an interrupted upload from restoring it, the service temporarily keeps a marker containing only the receipt identifier. The daily cleanup removes markers at least one day old, normally within two days of withdrawal. It cannot undo training or evaluation that was completed before withdrawal. Copies you download to your own device are under your control.

Site requests, local storage
and external services

Hosting. Your browser contacts Vercel to load the site and its assets. Request metadata can include network and browser information, time, path, selected classifier options and response status. The application uses a temporary in-memory rate limit. Vercel documents a one-hour runtime-log viewing window for our current Hobby plan. Infrastructure and security records are governed by Vercel’s own policies. Vercel runtime log limits · Vercel privacy notice

Browser storage. The Ocean application does not set advertising or analytics cookies. Clearing site data removes review notes saved in this browser. Downloaded files remain on your device until you delete them.

External services. Hugging Face receives a model-download request when you run the WHOI tool. Opening an OOI notebook moves your work to your signed-in Jupyter workspace, where files and results can be saved. External model demos and repositories have their own data practices.

Public contributions. The contribution form opens on GitHub. Submitting it publishes a public issue. Share links to openly available material or an access-request page. For restricted material, ask us about a suitable transfer method before sending files.

Using the results

Classifier suggestions and forecast outputs are provisional research results. Seek expert review before using them for operational, regulatory or safety decisions. Third-party data and models carry the licenses cited alongside them.

Contact SIG.AI Ocean

For a contribution or privacy question, a correction or a licensing concern, email ocean@sig.ai. To report a security issue privately, use the same address with “Security” in the subject line.
