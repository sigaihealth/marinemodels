Data and privacy

What happens to
your data.

Most tools on this site run in your browser. One sends an image to SIG.AI, and we keep the images it receives. This page explains what we keep, why, and how to ask us to delete it.

Everything else

Logs, cookies
and contributions

Request logs. Our hosting provider, Vercel, records standard details of each request, such as IP address, browser type and time, to run and secure the site. These logs are kept for [DECIDE: confirm the log retention period on your Vercel plan].

Stored images. Uploaded images are stored with [DECIDE: storage provider and region].

Cookies and analytics. This site doesn’t use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. The transect and V4277 review pages save your review decisions in your browser’s local storage, which never leaves your device. Clearing this site’s data removes them.

Contributions. The contribution form opens on GitHub and creates a public issue, so anything you post there is visible to everyone. GitHub’s own privacy statement applies. To share restricted material or ask something privately, email us instead.

Other sites. Links to archives, papers and model pages take you to sites with their own policies. Pages on this site don’t load content from those sites until you follow a link.

Using the results

Suggestions,
not identifications

Classifier suggestions and forecast outputs on this site are provisional. Treat them as candidates for expert review, not as the basis for operational, regulatory or safety decisions.

Images, data and models from other organizations keep their own licenses, which are shown next to each item.

Contact

Write to us

Email ocean@sig.ai to delete an upload, share restricted data, correct something on the site, raise a licensing concern or ask a question you’d rather not post publicly.

To report a security problem, email the same address with “Security” in the subject line. Please don’t open a public GitHub issue for it.

SIG.AI Ocean is run by sig.ai Inc. The company’s privacy policy and terms of use also apply, including your rights to know about and delete your information. Where they differ from this page about the ocean site, this page applies. [DECIDE: have counsel confirm this precedence sentence]
