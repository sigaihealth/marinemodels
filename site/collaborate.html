Ways to contribute

Bring a source
or a question.

Datasets
Link a DOI or access page. Describe the instrument, dates, broad region, units, and quality flags so someone can find comparable observations.
Models
Link a versioned model card with input and output examples, known limits, and the terms for using the checkpoint.
Runs and findings
Share a notebook or commit, the data version, and the result you want someone to reproduce. An unsuccessful replication can be as useful as a positive result.
Reviews and requests
Point to the specific claim, source, or image. Describe a check, a correction, or the expertise you need. Reviewers can link back to their independent work.

How it works

From record
to review

  1. Link the work. Submit its public landing page with the scientific question, version, access terms, and preferred citation. For restricted material, share a public access-request page. Keep private files, restricted weights, and precise sensitive locations out of public issues.
  2. Choose your visibility. The issue is public immediately. You can also invite a credited listing or independent replication. These choices leave the source license in force.
  3. Discuss the evidence. Researchers can reply with a correction, reproduction attempt, or related observation. For example, a reviewer can inspect the Pioneer samples and share which images would benefit from a second opinion.
