    Reading a result

    The Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution Sosik Lab trained this model on Northeast U.S. Shelf IFCB imagery. Its vocabulary includes organisms, mixed groups, debris, and imaging artifacts. The model assigns a score to every category even when an image contains an unfamiliar particle.

    This implementation converts the crop to grayscale, resizes it to 299 × 299 pixels with bicubic interpolation, and runs the frozen ONNX model. The JSON records the image fingerprint, model revision, and the three leading suggestions. For original observatory images, it also links the public source.
