    What it learned

    We fitted a nearest-centroid classifier to the WHOI Northeast U.S. Shelf dataset. It learns each class’s average crop size, grayscale distribution, contrast and particle shape after standardizing the features using training images.

    Acquisition bins are separate between training and validation. Cruises, dates and instruments can recur. These validation scores describe the balanced WHOI subset. The Pioneer examples need independent expert labels to measure transfer performance.
